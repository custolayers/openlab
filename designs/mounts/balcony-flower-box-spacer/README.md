# Spacer for Balcony Flower Box

A 3D-printed spacer that corrects the mounting geometry of Lechuza balcony flower box brackets to ensure straight, level installation on incompatible railings.

## Overview

This design is a custom spacer for **Lechuza balcony flower box mounting brackets**.  
It compensates for balcony railings whose shape or thickness does not match the assumptions of the stock Lechuza brackets.

Without this spacer, the flower boxes hang **crooked**, resulting in an unbalanced and visually unappealing installation.  
By introducing a precisely sized offset, the spacer restores proper alignment so the flower box sits straight and stable.

## Features

- Corrects misalignment caused by non-standard balcony railings
- Designed specifically for Lechuza balcony mounting brackets
- Optimized for PETG to withstand outdoor conditions
- Simple, tool-free integration with existing hardware

## Design Intent

The core idea was to **fix an aesthetic and mechanical issue without modifying the original Lechuza hardware**.

Design goals and constraints:
- Must fit seamlessly between the bracket and the railing
- Must be strong and weather-resistant for permanent outdoor use
- Must be easy to print on a standard FDM printer
- No permanent changes to the flower box or bracket system

The spacer geometry was iteratively adjusted to eliminate tilt while maintaining full load-bearing contact.

## Assembly

1. Remove the Lechuza mounting bracket from the balcony railing.
2. Place the printed spacer between the bracket and the railing at the contact point.
3. Reinstall and tighten the bracket as usual, ensuring the flower box sits level.

## Notes & Tips

- PETG is strongly recommended due to UV exposure and moisture
- Print with the largest flat surface on the build plate for strength
- Do not overtighten screws to avoid compressing the spacer
- Spacer is designed for a specific railing profile; verify fit before final installation
